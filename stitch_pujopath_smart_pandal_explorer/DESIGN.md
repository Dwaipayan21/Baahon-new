## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet anticipation of Bengal’s autumn festival season—encapsulated by *Maa Asche*—into a calm, highly legible mobile utility. Moving away from the visual excess, saturated red-and-gold gradients, and dense ornament often associated with festive apps, the aesthetic is rooted in the serene clarity of early Sharodotsav mornings: vast cerulean skies, drifts of white *kash phool* grasses, and crisp early-morning sunlight.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Soft Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- **Purity over Clutter:** Broad negative space, luminous pale-sky backdrops, and cloud-soft card elevations create visual respite during hectic festival navigation.
- **Effortless Single-Hand Ergonomics:** Prioritizes deep-mobile thumb reach through bottom sheets, expansive hit zones, and prominent tactile floating panels.
- **Restrained Reverence:** Warm brass and marigold accents are treated with strict editorial restraint, reserved exclusively for active states, festive highlights, and route triumphs to retain emotional impact.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, expansive atmosphere grounded by sharp, accessible slate typography.

### Core Tones
- **Primary (`#257CE6`):** Vibrant autumn morning sky. Governs active routes, primary action buttons, wayfinding pins, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#E2E8F0`):** Soft mist blue-gray. Serves as structural line dividers, inactive borders, and unselected state containers.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm marigold amber. Reserved strictly for subtle festival markers (e.g., featured pandal badges, cultural notes, rating stars).
- **Neutral Primary (`#0F172A`):** Midnight navy slate. Imparts solid typographic contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#64748B`):** Muted sky slate for secondary metadata, walking times, and pandal zones.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Soft Morning Tint (`#F2F8FD`) provides an expansive atmospheric canvas.
- **Card Surface:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) produces lift, mimicking cloud structures floating over morning skies.
- **Surface Variant:** Morning Dew (`#EBF5FF`) for non-urgent card highlights, route chips, and active filters.

### Functional Status (Crowd Levels)
- **Low Congestion:** Gentle Emerald (`#10B981`)
- **Moderate Congestion:** Muted Amber (`#F59E0B`)
- **High Congestion:** Soft Tangerine (`#F97316`)
- **Extreme / Peak Congestion:** Crimson Coral (`#EF4444`)

## Typography

The type system relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, friendly open counters, and high x-height for outdoor legibility in varying daytime sunlight.

### Typographic Principles
- **Airy Line Spacing:** Body copy uses generous proportional line heights (1.5x–1.6x) to preserve calm scanning during on-foot transit.
- **Numerical Weight:** Distance metrics, walking times, and transit steps lean on semi-bold and bold weights to ensure glanceability.
- **Editorial Micro-Copy:** Small metadata labels employ slight positive tracking (+0.02em to +0.04em) and medium/bold weights to guarantee readability on compact badges.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first architectural grid engineered for outdoor pedestrian usage and single-handed thumb operation.

### Breakpoints & Layout Model
- **Mobile (Base, 360px – 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Map canvas spans full bleed; all interactive UI elements sit within floating cards or bottom sheets.
- **Tablet (600px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins. Navigation moves to a docked split panel (380px fixed route explorer, remainder for map viewport).
- **Desktop / Large Tablet (1024px+):** 12-column layout. Explorer surfaces sit in a persistent floating column overlaying the right or left edge of the interactive map canvas.

### Vertical Rhythm & Thumb Zones
- Bottom navigation, floating action controls, and sheet drag handles must remain anchored inside the natural thumb arc (lower 40% of the viewport).
- Structural gaps default to `space-md` (`1rem`) between related list items and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) between independent spatial modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal, daytime ambient illumination. The elevation strategy relies on **soft, sky-tinted ambient diffusion** rather than dark muddy dropshadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat morning tint (`#F2F8FD`).
- **Level 1 (Inline Cards & List Units):** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate boundary ring: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Casts no harsh shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(37, 124, 230, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Buttons & Dynamic Pills):** Elevated white or primary surfaces with diffused blue lift: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 124, 230, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Route Overlays):** Grounded sheets layered over the map: `box-shadow: 0 -8px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 -2px 8px -1px rgba(37, 124, 230, 0.04)`.

### Backdrop Effects
Overlaid banners, filter bars, and contextual toolbars utilize frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), creating the impression of morning mist over the path map.

## Shapes

The shape profile is gentle and tactile, using smooth organic curves that evoke cloud edges and soft petals.

### Radius Assignments
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Badges, compact crowd indicators, and inline metadata tags.
- **Containers & Content Cards (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Pandal profile summary cards, search dropdown lists, and route segment items.
- **Prominent Shells & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Interactive bottom sheets (top corners only), prominent floating route modals, and map preview widgets.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter pills, category toggles, drag handles, and primary circular action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Targets
- **Primary CTA:** Minimum height of `48px` (52px optimal for thumb triggers). Solid `#257CE6` background, crisp white label (`label-lg`), pill shape (`rounded-full`), with `space-lg` horizontal padding. Subtle active press scale (`scale-98`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`, text in `#0F172A`. On active state, shifts surface to `#EBF5FF`.
- **Floating Map Action Buttons (Locate, Compass, Recenter):** `48x48px` circular containers (`rounded-full`), white background, Level 2 elevation, centered `#0F172A` iconography.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Heights fixed at `36px` for filters, `28px` for compact detail badges.
- **Inactive:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#64748B` typography.
- **Active:** `#EBF5FF` surface, `1.5px solid #257CE6`, text colored `#257CE6` in `label-md`.

### Cards (Pandal & Itinerary)
- Surface is crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with `rounded-xl` borders and Level 1 elevation.
- Internal padding is strict `space-md` (`1rem`).
- Cards host an image preview with a soft `rounded-lg` inner frame, accompanied by an explicit crowd level chip situated in the top-right corner.

### Crowd Density Indicators
- Compact capsule components (`height: 24px`, padding `0 8px`) composed of a 6px circular solid pip alongside an uppercase micro-label (`label-sm`).
- Example: Gentle Emerald (`#10B981`) text and dot sitting atop an ultra-soft tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`).

### Bottom Sheet Component
- Features an effortless draggable header with an unobtrusive pill drag indicator (`36px x 4px`, color `#94A3B8`, anchored `8px` from sheet top).
- Anchored snap states: Peek (`88px`), Half-screen (`45vh`), Full inspection (`92vh`).
- Contains independent internal scrolling with elastic deceleration.

### Search & Form Inputs
- `48px` minimum height container, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus state shifts border to `#257CE6` with a soft outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 124, 230, 0.15)`.
- Leading icon slot dedicated to wayfinding or search icons in `#64748B`.
